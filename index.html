A 20-page informed consent form is not a document. It is a wall — and behind it sits a patient who has eight minutes with a coordinator and a signature line they do not understand.

Aurobindo Pharma runs high-volume generics trials across antibiotics and CNS — both franchises where ethics committees have begun flagging readability as a protocol risk. The current ICFs average a 14.2 Flesch-Kincaid grade level. The patient average is eighth grade.

The FDA, EMA, and India's CDSCO have all issued draft guidance in the last 18 months signaling that signature-only consent is no longer defensible — patients must demonstrate comprehension. Without a defensible comprehension score per patient, the next IRB cycle is a delay. Without a delay avoided, the $45K cost stays on the books. This is the wedge.

Therapeutic Area Presets — Aurobindo's Three Franchises

Calibrated for Aurobindo's Generics, Antibiotics, and CNS reality.

Each preset carries its own target reading level, terminology dictionary, and IRB-facing template — built from Aurobindo's top three franchises. CNS targets the lowest grade (7) because cognitive load matters most; antibiotics targets grade 8 because the consent event is shorter; generics targets grade 9 for the broadest cohort.

PRESET 01

Generics

Broad-cohort, multilingual generics trials. Higher volume, shorter consent events. Reading-level target calibrated to the broadest patient population across India, EU, and US sites.

Target Grade 9.0
  • Bioequivalence terminology dictionary
  • Multi-language output (EN + HI + TE)
  • Standard risk-language template
  • Generic comparator language
PRESET 02

Antibiotics

Anti-infective trials where resistance, side-effect, and duration language dominates. Consent events are shorter but risk language is more pointed — preset enforces specific phrasing around resistance and superinfection.

Target Grade 8.0
  • Resistance terminology simplified
  • Side-effect lexicon (GI, dermatologic, systemic)
  • Duration-of-therapy plain-language patterns
  • Superinfection risk language
PRESET 03 · DEFAULT

CNS

Central nervous system trials where patient cognitive load is the constraint. Lowest target grade (7) because the cohort may include patients with depression, anxiety, or cognitive impairment. The highest-risk franchise for IRB readability flags.

Target Grade 7.0
  • Cognitive-load-aware sentence splitting
  • Psychiatric terminology dictionary
  • Capacity-to-consent plain-language checks
  • Washout and taper language simplified
